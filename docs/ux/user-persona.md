# User Persona: Alex

## Background

- **Age:** 32
- **Scenario:** Attending a Brighton & Hove Albion home match
- **Primary device:** Smartphone
- **Digital confidence:** Comfortable using apps and websites
- **Attendance:** Regular match attendee

## Goals

- Find important matchday information quickly.
- Check the latest match and stadium updates.
- Access information without navigating through multiple pages.
- Plan the journey to the stadium and know what to expect on arrival.

## Frustrations

- Websites that take too long to load.
- Information that doesn't update quickly enough.
- Having to search through menus to find relevant information.
- Uncertainty about whether the information displayed is current.

## Needs

- A fast, mobile-friendly experience.
- Important matchday information presented clearly.
- Current updates with clear timestamps where appropriate.
- Simple navigation and easy access to frequently used information.

## Design Opportunities

Albion Fan Hub could explore a personalised matchday dashboard that prioritises relevant information, reduces unnecessary navigation and makes the freshness of updates clear.

## Evidence and Assumptions

This is an initial design hypothesis, not a verified research participant. These assumptions should be tested and refined through supporter interviews, usability testing or other research where possible.
